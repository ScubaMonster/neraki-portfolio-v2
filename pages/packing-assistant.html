01_THE_CONTEXT

The trip is exciting. Preparing for it often is not.

Packing looks like a simple checklist task until destination, duration, weather, activities, luggage limits and personal needs begin competing for attention.

Generic lists can offer a starting point, but they still leave travellers to decide what applies, what is missing and how much of everything they actually need.

02_THE_GOAL

Turn travel details into a plan that feels personal.

Packing Assistant aims to ask only the questions that matter, generate a useful first draft and keep every recommendation transparent and editable.

The assistant should reduce remembering—not remove the traveller’s control.

03_THE_CHALLENGE

One suitcase can contain dozens of small decisions.

The concept focuses on the moments where preparation becomes repetitive, uncertain or easy to forget.

01

Mental overload

Travellers have to remember essentials while also planning transport, accommodation, activities and timing.

02

Generic advice

Standard packing lists rarely account for the specific trip, traveller, routine or luggage restrictions.

03

Changing conditions

Weather, plans and trip details can change after the first list is made, so the plan needs to adapt without starting again.

04_PLANNED_APPROACH

A product journey built around useful questions.

05_THE_CORE_FLOW

From trip profile to packing progress.

The intended flow collects a small set of meaningful details, creates a grouped list and lets the traveller adjust every item before tracking progress.

  • Add destination, dates and luggage type
  • Select activities and personal priorities
  • Review transparent recommendations
  • Add, remove, edit and check off items

07_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES

Three rules for a genuinely helpful assistant.

01

Ask with purpose.

Every question should visibly improve the list. If it does not, the traveller should not have to answer it.

02

Explain suggestions.

Recommendations should connect to the trip details that produced them, especially weather and planned activities.

03

Keep people in control.

Generated does not mean fixed. Every category and item should remain easy to edit, remove or personalise.
