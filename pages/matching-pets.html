Matching Pets

Helping adopters find the right companion.

A personality-based pet adoption experience designed to reduce decision fatigue, clarify compatibility and help people make thoughtful, confident choices.

01_THE_CONTEXT

Adoption is emotional. The decision still needs structure.

Pet-adoption platforms often ask people to browse long lists of animals and decide from limited information. That can create emotional overload while leaving important compatibility questions unanswered.

Matching Pets reframes the experience around lifestyle, needs and personality. Instead of asking users to search harder, it helps them narrow the field with more confidence.

02_THE_GOAL

Create a warmer, clearer path to a suitable match.

The goal was to design an approachable experience that balances emotion with practical information, supports informed decisions and makes the adoption journey feel less overwhelming.

The right match is not only about which pet someone likes. It is also about which relationship can realistically thrive.

03_THE_CHALLENGE

Three problems shaped the experience.

Each design decision connects back to clarity, compatibility and responsible adoption.

01

Too much browsing

Large pet lists make it difficult to compare meaningful traits and can turn the experience into endless scrolling.

02

Too little guidance

Users may not know which questions matter when evaluating energy, routine, environment and care needs.

03

High emotional stakes

The interface needs warmth and hope without encouraging rushed decisions based only on appearance.

05_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE

Organising the journey around decisions, not pages.

The journey preserves the possibility of “love at first sight” while offering guidance to people who are unsure what to ask. Both paths lead back to honest profiles and a realistic compatibility check.

  • Free browsing through playful personality categories
  • Optional guidance through lifestyle questions
  • Prioritised suggestions—not guaranteed certainty
  • Honest profiles, including more demanding cases

06_DESIGN_EVOLUTION

Show the thinking—not only the polished ending.

A prompt-generated Figma Make concept offered the first visual interpretation. Its logo and typography influenced the project, while the palette, structure and interface evolved during front-end implementation.

07_THE_SOLUTION

A friendly interface that makes compatibility easier to understand.

The final direction combines a warm visual identity with guided questions, clear hierarchy, concise content and responsive layouts.

Guided matching

Questions focus attention on lifestyle and care needs instead of leaving users to guess what matters.

Clear match signals

Results communicate fit in a simple, reassuring format that supports comparison without pretending certainty.

Scannable profiles

Important traits and care information are prioritised so users can understand each pet more quickly.

Responsive structure

Layouts adapt across screens while maintaining hierarchy, readability and comfortable interaction targets.

08_ACCESSIBILITY

Clarity is part of the experience—not a final decoration.

The interface uses semantic structure, visible labels, readable text, clear focus states and consistent patterns to reduce effort and support different ways of navigating.

  • Readable hierarchyClear headings, labels and content grouping.
  • Keyboard awarenessInteractive elements designed with focus visibility in mind.
  • Contrast checksText and controls remain legible against the visual system.
  • Scannable contentShort sections help reduce cognitive load.

09_REFLECTION

What this mission taught me.

01

Warmth needs structure.

A friendly interface is most useful when the information beneath it is carefully prioritised and easy to act on.

02

Matching should explain itself.

Users need to understand why a recommendation appears and what information still requires human judgement.

03

Design and code inform each other.

Building the interface exposed layout and content decisions that were easier to refine in a working responsive environment.
